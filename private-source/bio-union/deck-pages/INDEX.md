## Publication handling

### Strong potential evidence

These captures contain original-looking project evidence that may be suitable for extraction after ownership and accuracy review:

- `slide-19-garment-prototype.png`
- `slide-20-leap-m-hardware.png`
- `slide-36-medica-2017.png`

Prefer the original source photographs or design files over crops from the pitch deck whenever they are available.

### Reference-only by default

Keep these private unless their underlying sources and rights are cleared:

- Medical statistics and diagrams
- Consumer-watch product images
- Stock-model composites
- Market charts and forecasts
- Third-party logos
- Team portraits and names
- Interview, conference, or video-call screenshots
- ECG traces without source and test context
- Compliance badges and validation statements

### Required captions for recreated material

- `System diagram reconstructed in 2026 from Bio Union's 2023 product architecture.`
- `Historical positioning reconstructed from Bio Union's 2023 competitive analysis.`
- `Interface visualization recreated from archived concepts and development materials.`
- `2026 retrospective concept - not part of the original Bio Union product.`

## Agent rule

The build agent may inspect this folder locally but must not import from it in production code.

Before using material represented here:

1. Locate the original underlying asset if possible.
2. Add it to the Bio Union verification register.
3. Confirm ownership, consent, accuracy, and claim status.
4. Create a web-ready derivative in a separate approved asset-preparation area.
5. Add only the approved derivative to the public repository.
6. Record the public asset's provenance and caption.

If verification is incomplete, use a `TODO(content)` placeholder or a newly reconstructed diagram with an explicit disclosure. Do not publish the private slide capture.
